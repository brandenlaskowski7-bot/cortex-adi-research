**Author:** Branden Laskowski  
**Canonical external preprint:** [Zenodo record 23990079](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23990079)  
**DOI supplied in the author's public publication notice:** `10.5281/zenodo.23990079`

This is the **Paper Five** record in the ADI research series. It introduces the Cortex Continuum Substrate as the persistent governed continuity boundary external to model inference: memory continuity, authorization scope, provenance, and auditability do not depend on the lifetime or identity of any particular AI model or client.

The author's public release notice reports controlled first-party synthetic experiments: 12/12 frozen fixtures, 23/23 kernel tests, 13/13 security-boundary tests, an encrypted restart and provenance check, 900/900 staged local retrieval operations, 50/50 post-restart recoveries, and 25 adversarial cases with no observed unauthorized effects. **These are bounded first-party claims, not independent replication, production certification or universal portability.** See the canonical Zenodo publication for the publication's exact wording and attached evidence.
