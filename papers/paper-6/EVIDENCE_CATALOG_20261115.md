# Paper 6 evidence catalog — 15 November 2026

This catalog is the evidence chain for **The Future of AI and Institutional
Memory: Model-Independent Scoped Memory**. It follows the system outward:
record lifecycle and continuity; institutional-scale deterministic handling;
authorized retrieval boundaries; model and machine substitution; then hostile
or negative results. Every entry is first-party and synthetic unless stated
otherwise. A pass is evidence for the stated bounded protocol only.

No personal or customer records, secrets, credentials, raw request bodies, or
non-public implementation topology are included here.

## How to read the catalog

* **Evidence class** says what was actually exercised. It is not a maturity
  label.
* **Supports** is the maximum defensible conclusion.
* **Does not establish** prevents a local/synthetic result from becoming a
  production, security-certification, or universal-model claim.
* The machine-readable [catalog](evidence/CATALOG.json) supplies source paths
  and SHA-256 values for included primary result files.

## Cross-cutting conclusion

Together, these records support a **research proposition**, not a blanket
product guarantee: institutions can place canonical record authority,
lifecycle, provenance, and release decisions in deterministic systems outside
changing AI models. Models and clients can then consume only released,
scope-bound working views. The included negative evidence demonstrates why the
proposal must be evaluated as a protocol with retained failures—not as a claim
that a model or a single benchmark is inherently trustworthy.
