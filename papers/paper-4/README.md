# Deterministic Worker Construction Before Inference

**ADI Research Series Paper 4**  
Branden Laskowski · Cortex Agentics Global

This is the fourth paper in the ADI Research Series. It is currently an unpublished manuscript prepared for future public preprint submission.

- **Status:** Manuscript prepared; not yet submitted to Zenodo
- **DOI:** Not assigned
- **Evidence companion:** `ADI_Paper_4_Public_Safe_Evidence_Companion.zip`

The paper studies whether stable worker state can be constructed before model inference so that repeated model input can be reduced on a bounded synthetic workload. The reported evidence is limited to the stated benchmark, methods, and limitations. It does not establish universal quality preservation, general model equivalence, production savings, or disclosure of proprietary implementation.

The public Paper 4 manuscript and evidence companion are maintained separately from the submitted Paper 3 release.
