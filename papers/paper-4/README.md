# Behavioral Governance of Artificial Workers

**ADI Research Series Paper 4**  
Branden Laskowski · Cortex Agentics Global

This is the fourth paper in the ADI Research Series.

- **Status:** Public preprint
- **DOI:** [10.5281/zenodo.23887999](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23887999)
- **Public record:** [Zenodo](https://zenodo.org/records/23887999)
- **Evidence boundary:** Public claims are limited to the stated methods, results, and limitations. Private implementation, raw trial material, and worker packages are not distributed here.

The paper examines whether a governed artificial worker can be evaluated not only for task completion, but also for compliance with its declared authority, scope, and behavioral requirements before a completion is released. The system-level claim is bounded: external controls can evaluate the stated acceptance contract in the tested setting. It does not establish universal behavioral safety, general intelligence equivalence, or disclosure of proprietary implementation.
