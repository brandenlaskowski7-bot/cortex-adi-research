## How to use this ledger

- Cite the listed **current version DOI** for a specific released version.
- Use a Zenodo **concept DOI** only when citing the record family as a whole.
- Treat the “public evidence” column as a boundary: it says what a public reader can inspect, not what private evidence exists.
- A published paper is not independent replication, peer-review acceptance, product certification, or proof of performance outside its stated test conditions.

## Private evidence
