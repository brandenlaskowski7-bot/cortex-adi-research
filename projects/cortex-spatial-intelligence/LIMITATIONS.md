# Limitations

## Public reference limitations

- The synthetic graph is small and single-floor.
- The reference uses standard deterministic graph procedures and does not reproduce private production algorithms.
- No real-time positioning provider is connected.
- No accessibility, congestion, closure, hazard, multi-floor, or equipment overlay is executed in the public app.
- No production performance, latency, concurrency, availability, or security claim is made.
- The public promotion reference does not implement the production frequency-history model or customer-preference model.
- The Space is not a warehouse-management system.
- No real retailer integration, map ingestion, merchandise feed, or mobile application is included.

## Evidence limitations

- The public 22-test suite directly verifies only the independent public reference and synthetic fixture.
- Private production test totals are reported as provenance, not as independently reproducible public evidence.
- Passing deterministic unit tests does not establish operational savings, sales lift, accessibility outcomes, or production fitness.
- No independent third-party review or replication is claimed at release-candidate stage.

## Commercial and legal limitations
