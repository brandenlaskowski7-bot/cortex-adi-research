# Claims and limitations

## What ADI proposes

ADI is an architectural synthesis: inference generates bounded proposals while external systems decide authority-bearing transitions under explicit state and policy. Determinism is conditional on fully specified inputs, relevant state, event order, time, and the trusted computing base. This vocabulary does not establish novelty over existing security, verification, or governed-agent work.

## What the published paper reports

The paper reports bounded first-party synthetic observations. In its repeated memory study, 30 designs repeated three times per arm produced 90/90 accepted jobs for both scoped 8B and scoped 35B configurations, 72/90 for scoped 4B, and 46/90 for broader-context 35B. The broader-context condition had 43 truncations under its fixed output budget. Repetitions are not 90 independent task designs, and this comparison does not isolate a causal effect of model size or architecture.

The separate eight-task context-admission diagnostic retains PARTIAL overall parity after its context correction. Historical test results are historical evidence; worker-construction performance candidates with incomplete artifact lineage remain excluded. Negative and held results remain part of the research record.

## What is not established

- Universal model equivalence, superior general reasoning, or transfer of all competence outside a model.
- Complete mediation of every effect channel, universal safety, truthful memory, or correct policies merely because decisions are deterministic.
- Full temporal enforcement, production readiness, long-term identity continuity, or a completed live model-swap study.
- Educational benefit, validated personality fidelity, consciousness, personhood, or safe robotic embodiment.
- Independent replication, independent human review, peer-review acceptance, or public reproducibility of the private raw corpus.

## Reproducibility and criticism

This repository provides the paper, public framing, and a proposed behavioral challenge stub. It contains no implementation or runnable benchmark. Private companion artifacts mentioned in the PDF are not accessible here. A hash proves file identity, not the truth of an experiment. An independently reproducible behavioral package needs a separate approved specification, synthetic fixtures, oracle, environment details, and preserved results.

Useful challenges include an optimized authorized-retrieval baseline, matched output budgets, unseen task designs, alternative failure interpretations, temporal boundary cases, and demonstrations of unmediated effects. Report evidence validity, task quality, authority enforcement, continuity, and reproducibility separately.
